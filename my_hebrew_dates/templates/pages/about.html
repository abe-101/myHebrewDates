About My Hebrew Dates

My Hebrew Dates puts Hebrew birthdays, anniversaries, and yahrzeits into the calendar you already use, on the right English date every year.

Why I built it

My grandmother kept telling me she couldn't keep track of her grandchildren's birthdays. She wanted to send each of them a gift on the day, but Hebrew birthdays move around the English calendar every year, and her calendar couldn't follow them.

In 2023 I sat down with a friend who knows the ins and outs of calendars. I knew Django, he knew how the Hebrew and English calendars line up, and by the end of that session we had a working prototype. That prototype grew into My Hebrew Dates.

— Abe Hanoka

How it works

You add each date once, by its Hebrew date. My Hebrew Dates works out the English date for every year and serves it as a calendar link. Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook subscribe to that link, so the dates show up with a reminder and stay up to date when you change them. Family members can subscribe to the same link without an account.

Free and open source

My Hebrew Dates is free to use, and the code is open source on GitHub. Questions, bug reports, and ideas are always welcome.
