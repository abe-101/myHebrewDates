How to get a yahrzeit reminder every year

A yahrzeit is kept on the Hebrew date of a person's passing, so it falls on a different English date each year. It begins at sunset the evening before, which is when the yahrzeit candle is usually lit.

Find the Hebrew date

If you know only the English date of passing, use the Hebrew date converter. If the passing was after sunset, tick After sunset, because the Hebrew day had already changed.

Set up a reminder

  1. Sign up and make a calendar.
  2. Add the person, choose Yahrzeit, and enter the Hebrew date.
  3. On the calendar's Add to your calendar page, pick a reminder time. Because the yahrzeit begins the evening before, a reminder such as 7 PM the evening before gives you time to light the candle.
  4. Choose Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook to subscribe.

From then on, the yahrzeit appears on the right day every year, and family members you share the link with can subscribe and pick their own reminder time.

Adar and leap years

In a Hebrew leap year there are two months of Adar. Customs differ on which one to use for a passing in a year with a single Adar, so ask your rabbi if you're unsure, then choose Adar I or Adar II when you add the date.
