Apple Calendar repeats events by the English calendar, so a Hebrew birthday or yahrzeit set to repeat yearly ends up on the wrong day. Subscribing to a calendar that follows the Hebrew dates keeps every date on the right day, year after year.

Steps

  1. Sign up and make a calendar for your family.
  2. Add each person's Hebrew birthday, anniversary, or yahrzeit. If you only know the English date, enter that and the Hebrew date is filled in for you.
  3. On your iPhone, open the calendar's Add to your calendar page and tap Apple Calendar.
  4. Your iPhone asks whether to subscribe. Confirm, and the dates appear in the Calendar app.

The same steps work on an iPad or a Mac. Calendars you subscribe to on one device may also appear on your others if you use iCloud.

Sharing with family

Send family members the link to the calendar's Add to your calendar page. They don't need an account. They tap their calendar app and subscribe, and see every date you add.
